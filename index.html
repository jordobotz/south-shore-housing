Reading the badges

PrimaryAvailability was read on the property's own site, its manager or listing agent, or a state registry, within the last two weeks.
SecondaryRead on a reputable third party (a lottery administrator, a town plan).
Aggregator onlyOnly an apartment-listing aggregator mentions it. Treat as a lead.
ConflictTwo equally good sources disagree, usually on availability. A phone call settles it.
NoFound in a registry but nothing about it could be confirmed yet.
Available nowA unit was listed as open on the day checked.
Waitlist openApplications accepted; wait length usually unknown until you call.
Now ✓A checkmark on the move-in band means a move-in is plausible inside the 3-month window; the band itself (Now, ≤3 mo, 3–12 mo) is how long the wait is.

"~30% of income" means rent is set by the program from household income, so the dollar figure is an estimate for a household of two with income of about $78k, not a listed price. "Min income (2p)" is a floor some fixed-rent buildings require; a household below it does not qualify.

Built from public registries (Housing Navigator MA, MassHousing, HUD, town housing inventories) and each property's own site. Availability changes weekly; the date checked is on every record. Nothing here is an application; every listing links to the place that takes one.
