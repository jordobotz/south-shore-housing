What to ask on every call

  1. Is it still available, and could we move in by December?
  2. Is it a year-round lease, or a winter rental that ends in the spring? Is it furnished?
  3. How many off-street parking spaces come with it — two? Is any parking on the street, and is there a winter parking ban?
  4. What floor is it on, and how many stairs? Is there an elevator?
  5. How big is it, in square feet?
  6. Is there a washer and dryer in the apartment? A dishwasher?
  7. Is there storage — a basement, a shed, or a walk-in closet?
  8. What is the rent, and what does it include? Is there an income requirement?
